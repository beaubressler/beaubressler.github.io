US Price-Rent Ratios

Explore housing affordability across ZIP codes (2015-2025)

The price-rent ratio shows how many years of rent it would take to buy a home. A ratio of 20 means a home costs about 20 years of rent. Lower ratios suggest buying is more affordable; higher ratios suggest renting may be more economical.

Understanding Price-Rent Ratios

0-10: Very low
10-15: Low
15-20: Moderate
20-25: High
25+: Very high

About the Data

Data sources: Zillow Home Value Index (ZHVI) for home prices and Zillow Observed Rent Index (ZORI) for rental prices. Coverage expands from ~1,000 ZIP codes in 2015 to ~7,000 ZIP codes in 2025 as Zillow's data collection improved over time.
